## Feature spotlights

Use one per post for a week of content.

**1. Gear = the board**
> In GearLink Battle, your loadout *is* the board. Every piece of gear you equip becomes an orb colour. Choose your five wisely.

**2. Bombs**
> Link 6 or more and you forge a bomb. Tap it to blast a 3×3 area, and every orb caught fires its gear. Bombs set off bombs. 💥

**3. The heroes**
> 🛡️ **Hero**: +30% block, starts every fight with a shield
> 🏹 **Archer**: +25% attack
> 🔮 **Mage**: +50% healing
> Which one are you maining?

**4. Riders**
> Long links unlock riders.
> 🔥 Burn ticks every turn
> 🎯 Mark sets up a ×2.25 hit
> ❄️ Frost freezes the enemy's swing
> A Common with the right rider can out-hit an Epic.

**5. Duels**
> No monsters. No turns. Two boards on the same clock.
> Link 5+ to dump junk on your opponent's grid. Run out of moves and you lose on the spot.
> Climb from Bronze to Knight every week. ⚔️

**6. Ascension**
> Beat The King and the whole road starts again: enemies tougher, scores bigger. How high can you ascend?

**7. Heart containers**
> Bosses drop heart pieces. Three make a container. Each one is +4 max HP for the hero you choose. Build your main.
