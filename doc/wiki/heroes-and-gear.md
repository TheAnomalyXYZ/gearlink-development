# Heroes & Gear

## Heroes

Your hero's perk lasts the whole run.

| Hero | Perk | Plays like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero** | +30% block, starts every battle with 10 block | Tanky. Soaks hits and outlasts the enemy. |
| **Archer** | +25% attack damage | Glass cannon. Kills fast, builds up **Mark** for huge hits. |
| **Mage** | +50% healing | Sustain. Wears enemies down with **Burn** and stalls them with **Frost**. |

Every hero starts with **40 HP**. You can raise that permanently with [heart containers](campaign.md#heart-containers).

## Loadout rules

- Equip **exactly five** pieces.
- All five must belong to **your hero's class**.
- **At most 2 of any one type** (attack, block, heal).
- You can only equip gear you **own**. New accounts start with a default five for every hero, so you can play right away.

Each piece becomes one orb colour on the board, so your loadout literally decides what the board is made of.

## Rider effects

Many cards carry a **rider**: a bonus that only fires when the link is **long enough**. That's what stops the loadout from being "just take the highest Power". A Common with a good rider can beat an Epic without one.

| Rider | Lands on | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔥 **Burn** | Enemy | Deals that much damage at the end of every turn, then drops by 1. Can finish an enemy off. |
| 🎯 **Mark** | Enemy | Your next attack link uses up a Mark stack: ×1.5 at 1 stack, ×1.75 at 2, ×2.25 at 3. Set it up, then cash it in. |
| ❄️ **Frost** | Enemy | Each stack stalls the enemy's charge meter for one turn, delaying its swing. |
| 💪 **Strength** | You | Permanent for the battle: every attack link hits for that much more. |
| 🛡️ **Grit** | You | Permanent for the battle: every block link gives that much more block. |

Buffs on you (Strength, Grit) land **before** the link pays out, so the link that grants them already benefits. Mark lands **after**, so a link never uses up the Mark it just applied.

Riders only fire from a **drawn link**, never from a bomb blast.

## Build tips

- **Archer + Mark:** Stack Mark with long links on Sylvan Plate or Rabbit Talisman, then cash it in with a big Zephyr Bow link for ×2.25 damage.
- **Mage + Burn:** Burn keeps ticking while you block and heal. Molten Amulet's Burn 5 on a 4-link is one of the strongest riders in the game.
- **Mage + Frost:** Frost Staff or Frozen Tome can freeze an elite's meter long enough to skip its heavy hit.
- **Hero + Strength:** Leather Helm's Strength 3 stacks for the whole battle. Get it early and every sword link after it hits harder. Pair with Dragon Crest's Grit for a shield that grows every link.
