# FAQ

**Do I need to buy anything to play?**
No. Every account starts with a full default loadout for all three heroes, plus 250 coins and 60 gems. Coins, gems and packs all come from playing: battles, quests and league prizes.

**Can diagonals be linked?**
Yes. Any direction counts, as long as each orb touches the previous one.

**Why did my run end when I still had HP?**
The board had no legal link left (no 3 matching orbs touching, and no bomb). A dead board ends the run.

**Does my HP come back between waves?**
No. A battle is one fight across all its waves. Plan your heals. Max HP goes up permanently with [heart containers](campaign.md#heart-containers).

**Why can't I equip three swords?**
Loadouts allow at most 2 of each type (attack, block, heal), and all five must belong to your hero.

**My Strength/Grit vanished.**
They last for the whole battle, across all its waves, but reset when the battle ends.

**Can I farm Greenwood for heart pieces?**
No. Heart pieces only drop on a location's **first** clear in each ascension.

**Is my duel opponent a real person?**
Sometimes. Real players who've entered the league appear with their own loadout and avatar, played by the AI on their behalf. House bots fill the gaps. Either way the match runs live against the AI on your device.

**What happens if I stop playing duels?**
At each Monday reset, level 1 of a tier drops to level 2 of the tier below. You'll settle into a tier rather than falling all the way down. You never drop below Bronze 1.

**Why did I get no league prize this week?**
You need at least 3 duels in a week to earn one.

**Can scores be faked?**
No. The server replays every move of your run through the same engine and works out the score itself. Edited scores, forged loadouts or tampered moves are rejected.

**When do things reset?**
Daily quests and the Daily Battle post: 00:00 UTC every day. Weekly quests and duel seasons: Monday 00:00 UTC.
