# GearLink Battle Wiki

GearLink Battle is a match-and-link roguelike you play right inside Reddit posts. Equip five pieces of gear, drag across matching orbs to attack, block and heal, and fight your way across the map to The King.

## The 30-second version

1. **Pick a place** on the map. Each location is a battle of 3–5 waves, and the last wave is always an elite boss.
2. **Pick a hero**: Hero (block), Archer (attack) or Mage (healing).
3. **Equip five gear pieces.** Each piece becomes one orb colour on the board.
4. **Drag through 3 or more orbs of the same colour** (diagonals count) to fire that piece. Red attacks, blue blocks, green heals.
5. **Watch the enemy's meter.** When it fills, the enemy swings. Block or heal before it does.
6. **Beat the boss** to take the location and unlock the next one. Beat The King at the Castle to **ascend** and start the road again, harder and worth more.
