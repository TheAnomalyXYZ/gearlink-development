# The Campaign

The campaign is a road of **six locations**. Each location is a short battle of 3–5 waves, and the **last wave is always an elite boss**. Beat the boss and the next location opens.

## Locations

| # | Location | Waves | Enemies | Boss | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Greenwood** (Forest) | 3 | Bandit Scout | Bandit Leader | Bandits on the road. Short, and it forgives a bad link. |
| 2 | **Old Bridge** | 3–4 | Bandit Scout, Bandit Leader | Bridge Troll | The toll is paid in HP. The troll hits like a wall falling. |
| 3 | **Dripstone Caves** | 3–4 | Cave Bat, Slime | Cave Mother Slime | Big pools, slow swings. Bring something that keeps hitting. |
| 4 | **Ghost Town** | 4–5 | Wandering Spirit, Poltergeist | Phantom Warlord | Five waves of dead men. Block is worth more than damage here. |
| 5 | **Frostspire** (Mountain) | 4–5 | Ice Golem, Frost Dragonling | Ice Queen | Armoured and patient. The Queen punishes a wasted turn. |
| 6 | **The Castle** | 4–5 | Castle Guard, Champion Knight | **The King** | The King holds the last wave. Put him down and you ascend. |

You can replay any location you've already unlocked.

## Affixes

Waves before the boss can roll an **affix** that changes how the fight plays. The first three waves of Greenwood are always plain, so new players learn one rule at a time.

| Affix | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Armored** | Cuts 35% off every attack that lands, however long the link. |
| **Brute** | Hits far harder (×1.8), but winds up a beat longer. |
| **Swarm** | Frail: a much smaller HP pool, but swings a beat sooner. |
| **Warden** | Caps how much block you can hold. |
| **Leech** | Halves everything your heal links restore. |
| **Elite** *(every boss)* | Tougher (+55% HP), stronger (+20%), and **every third hit is a heavy** (×1.5). |

**The King** is tougher still: +30% HP and +10% strength on top of being an elite. He's meant to end a run that arrives underbuilt.

## Heart containers

Your HP pool is the one stat you upgrade permanently.

- A location's boss drops **heart pieces** the **first time** you clear it in each ascension. Deeper bosses drop more:

  | Boss of | Heart pieces |
  | --- | --- |
  | Greenwood, Old Bridge | 1 |
  | Dripstone Caves, Ghost Town | 2 |
  | Frostspire, The Castle | 3 |

- **3 pieces make 1 heart container.**
- Each container gives **+4 max HP** to the **one hero** you apply it to.
- Pieces are a shared pool, so a drop is never wasted on a hero you don't play. Containers are per hero, so building one up is a real choice.
- Max **10 containers** per hero (40 → 80 HP).

## Ascension

Beat **The King** and you **ascend**: the map resets to Greenwood, one tier harder.

Each ascension compounds across the whole map:

| Per ascension | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Enemy HP | ×1.32 |
| Enemy strength | ×1.18 |
| Score | ×1.25 |

So climbing again is a bigger number, not the same one twice. Heart pieces drop again for each first clear in the new ascension. The ladder stops at **Ascension 9**.
