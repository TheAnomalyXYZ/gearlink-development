# Daily Battle

Every day at **00:00 UTC**, a fresh **Daily Battle** post goes up in the subreddit.

## How it works

- Each Daily Battle features **one foe** and the location it guards. The foe changes every day and jumps between regions, so no two days in a row feel the same. Every foe comes round once per cycle.
- Each post has its **own ladder**. Every day starts with an empty board and a crown nobody holds yet.
- Opening the post takes you **straight to that day's location**.

## Open to everyone

The day's location is open to every player **on that post**, even if you haven't unlocked it on your own map yet. That means a brand-new player can take a swing at the Castle on a Castle day.

A daily fight past your own progress:

- ✅ Counts for that post's ladder
- ✅ Earns coins
- ❌ Doesn't unlock locations on your map
- ❌ Doesn't drop heart pieces
- ❌ Doesn't count as an ascension

## The crown

The top score on the post's ladder holds the crown for the day. The post's preview in the feed shows the day's foe, the current top players and how many people have played.

## For moderators

- The Daily Battle posts automatically once a day.
- If it's missing, use the subreddit menu item **"Post today's Daily Battle"**. It only ever posts once per day, so it's safe to press.
- **"Create a GearLink Battle post"** makes a general post you can play from or pin.
