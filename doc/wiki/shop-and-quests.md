# Shop, Packs & Quests

## Currencies

| Currency | How you get it | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🪙 **Coins** | Battles (1 coin per 40 score), quests, daily rewards, league prizes | Base and Bronze Packs, [levelling up gear](heroes-and-gear.md#gear-levels) |
| 💎 **Gems** | Quests, daily rewards, league prizes, or bought with Reddit Gold | Silver and Gold Packs; trading for coins |

New accounts start with **250 coins** and **60 gems**.

## Packs

Packs are rolled on the server, so the odds shown are the real odds.

| Pack | Price | Cards | Tier | Guarantee | Common | Rare | Epic | Legendary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Base Pack** | 90 coins | 1 | Bronze | – | 70% | 25% | 5% | – |
| **Bronze Pack** | 450 coins | 1 | Bronze | Rare or better | – | 87% | 12% | 1% |
| **Silver Pack** | 100 gems | 1 | **Silver** | Epic or better | – | – | 97% | 3% |
| **Gold Pack** | 150 gems | 2 | **Gold** | Epic or better | – | – | 85% | 15% |

Each card picks its rarity first, then a random piece of that rarity from any hero. Legendaries are weapons only, so a Legendary pull is one of the three heroes' Legendary weapons.

Packs only give **base pieces**, never [variants](heroes-and-gear.md#variants).

### Pack tiers

A pack's tier decides what [gear tier](heroes-and-gear.md#gear-levels) its cards land at:

| You pull... | Bronze tier (Base, Bronze Packs) | Silver tier (Silver Pack) | Gold tier (Gold Pack) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A card you don't own** | New card at Bronze LV 1 | New card at **Silver LV 1** | New card at **Gold LV 1** |
| **A card you own, below the pack's tier** | – | **Promoted** to Silver LV 1, +1 spare copy | **Promoted** to Gold LV 1, +1 spare copy |
| **A card you own, at or above the pack's tier** | +1 spare copy | +1 spare copy, level kept | +1 spare copy, level kept |

Example: your Blessed Hammer (Epic) is at Bronze LV 7. A Silver Pack pulls it again, so it jumps to **Silver LV 1** and gains a spare copy. If it had already been at Silver LV 4, it would stay at Silver LV 4 and just gain the copy.

Silver and Gold cards show their tier on the reveal. A pull that promotes a card gets a **SILVER!** or **GOLD!** stamp instead of the usual duplicate crack.

### Duplicates

Pull a card you already own and it becomes a **spare copy** of that card. Spare copies and coins level the card up; see [Gear levels](heroes-and-gear.md#gear-levels).

Owning every piece of a gear set also unlocks a hero [outfit](outfits.md#gear-sets).

## Daily rewards & streaks

Open GearLink once a day and claim a login reward. Claim on back-to-back days (days reset at 00:00 UTC) to build a **streak**. The rewards run in a 7-day cycle that repeats:

| Day | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 50 coins |
| 2 | 80 coins |
| 3 | 5 gems |
| 4 | 120 coins |
| 5 | Base Pack |
| 6 | 150 coins + 5 gems |
| 7 | Bronze Pack + 10 gems, and a **shield** |

**Shields.** Every finished week earns a shield (you can hold 2). If you miss a day, a shield is spent automatically on your next claim and your streak carries on. Miss more days than you have shields and the streak restarts at day 1.

**Milestones.** Long streaks pay a one-off bonus on top of that day's reward:

| Streak | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 14 days | Silver Pack |
| 30 days | Gold Pack + 30 gems |
| 60 days | Gold Pack + 60 gems |
| 100 days | 2 Gold Packs + 100 gems |

The reward calendar pops up the first time you reach the home screen each day. You can also open it from the top of the **Quests** screen.

## Quests

Quests track what the server has already verified (banked runs, finished duels, opened packs), so progress is always real.

### Daily quests

Reset every day at **00:00 UTC**.

| Quest | Goal | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Take the Field** | Fight 3 battles on the map | 60 coins |
| **Hold the Line** | Win 2 battles | 100 coins |
| **Wave Breaker** | Clear 12 waves | 80 coins |
| **Long Link** | Link a chain of 8 in one battle | 5 gems |
| **Sparring Partner** | Play 2 duels | 80 coins |
| **Fresh Steel** | Open a pack | 60 coins |
| **Daily Duties** | Claim 4 other daily quests | Base Pack |

### Weekly quests

Reset every **Monday at 00:00 UTC**.

| Quest | Goal | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Campaigner** | Win 15 battles | Bronze Pack |
| **War Chest** | Bank 2,000 coins from battles | Silver Pack |
| **Duellist** | Win 10 duels | 25 gems |
| **Collector** | Open 6 packs | 20 gems |
| **Regicide** | Defeat The King | Gold Pack |
