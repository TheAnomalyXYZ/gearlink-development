# Shop, Packs & Quests

## Currencies

| Currency | How you get it | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🪙 **Coins** | Battles (1 coin per 40 score), quests, league prizes, duplicate refunds | Base Packs |
| 💎 **Gems** | Quests, league prizes, or bought with Reddit Gold | Bronze, Silver and Gold Packs; trading for coins |

New accounts start with **250 coins** and **60 gems**.

## Packs

Every pack gives **one gear card**. Packs are rolled on the server, so the odds are the odds.

| Pack | Price | Guarantee | Common | Rare | Epic | Legendary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Base Pack** | 90 coins | – | 70% | 25% | 5% | – |
| **Bronze Pack** | 60 gems | Rare or better | 45% | 40% | 13% | 2% |
| **Silver Pack** | 100 gems | Epic or better | 25% | 45% | 27% | 3% |
| **Gold Pack** | 150 gems | Epic or better | 10% | 40% | 42% | 8% |

If a pack rolls below its guarantee, it's bumped up to the guaranteed rarity. Since Legendaries are weapons only, a Legendary roll can land on any hero's Legendary.

### Duplicates

Pull a card you already own and it turns into coins:

| Rarity | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 25 coins |
| Rare | 60 coins |
| Epic | 150 coins |
| Legendary | 400 coins |

## Quests

Quests track what the server has already verified (banked runs, finished duels, opened packs), so progress is always real.

### Daily quests

Reset every day at **00:00 UTC**.

| Quest | Goal | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Take the Field** | Fight 3 battles on the map | 60 coins |
| **Hold the Line** | Win 2 battles | 100 coins |
| **Wave Breaker** | Clear 12 waves | 80 coins |
| **Long Link** | Link a chain of 8 in one battle | 5 gems |
| **Sparring Partner** | Play 2 duels | 80 coins |
| **Fresh Steel** | Open a pack | 60 coins |
| **Daily Duties** | Claim 4 other daily quests | Base Pack |

### Weekly quests

Reset every **Monday at 00:00 UTC**.

| Quest | Goal | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Campaigner** | Win 15 battles | Bronze Pack |
| **War Chest** | Bank 2,000 coins from battles | Silver Pack |
| **Duellist** | Win 10 duels | 25 gems |
| **Collector** | Open 6 packs | 20 gems |
| **Regicide** | Defeat The King | Gold Pack |
