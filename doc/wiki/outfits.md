# Outfits

Outfits change how your hero looks. They **never change stats**, so wear whatever you like.

Each hero has **8 outfits**: the Classic look plus 7 more to unlock. You pick an outfit for each hero separately, and the game remembers all three.

## How to change outfit

1. Open the **hero select** screen.
2. Pick a hero, then tap **OUTFIT** on their info panel. The number under it shows how many outfits that hero has (e.g. 3/8).
3. Tap **WEAR** on any outfit you own.

Locked outfits show as a dark silhouette with what it takes to unlock them. When you unlock a new one, a pop-up tells you straight away.

Once you unlock an outfit, it's yours for good, even if a new ascension sends you back to the start of the map.

## Gear sets

Own at least one copy of all five pieces in a set and its outfit unlocks. Gear comes from [packs](shop-and-quests.md#packs), and every piece in every set can drop from them.

Using copies to [level up gear](heroes-and-gear.md) never takes a set outfit away.

## Shop outfits

**Sakura** (Archer) and **Starweaver** (Mage) can only be bought, for **250 gems** each. You can't earn them by playing. You start wearing an outfit as soon as you buy it.

[Gems](shop-and-quests.md#currencies) come from quests, daily rewards and prizes, or can be bought with Reddit Gold.

## Where your outfit shows

Your outfit shows wherever your hero stands full-size: the hero select screen and the loadout screen. The small portraits in battles and duels still show each hero's Classic look.
