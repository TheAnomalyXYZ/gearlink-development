# Weekly Leaderboard

One leaderboard for the whole subreddit. Every campaign battle you fight, on any GearLink post, counts toward it. Tap the **trophy button** on the map to open it.

## Weekly points

Your weekly points are **your best battle score at each location this week, added up**.

- Deeper locations and higher ascensions score more for the same play, so climbing pays.
- Only a location's **best** counts. Replaying a fight only adds what it beats your old best by, so grinding one spot does very little.
- Losing battles still score. Points come from damage, waves cleared and your best link.

The board resets every **Monday at 00:00 UTC**, the same time as duel leagues and weekly quests.

## Titles and prizes

At the reset, everyone who played **3 or more battles** in the week gets a prize based on where they finished. The top 10 also take a **title**: a subreddit flair only they can wear, for the week after. When that week ends the flair comes off, unless you earn it again.

| Finish   | Title flair     | Prize                   | Needs                  |
| -------- | --------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| #1       | Weekly Champion | 600 coins + Silver Pack | 3+ players on the week |
| #2-3     | Weekly Top 3    | 400 coins + Bronze Pack | 6+ players             |
| #4-10    | Weekly Top 10   | 250 coins + Base Pack   | 15+ players            |
| Top 25%  | -               | 150 coins               | 8+ players             |
| Top 50%  | -               | 100 coins               | 4+ players             |
| Everyone | -               | 60 coins                | -                      |

- You get the best prize your finish qualifies for. If the week is too small for a tier, you drop to the next one down.
- Prizes land in your bag the first time you open the game after the reset. A notice tells you where you finished, and **WEAR IT** puts the title flair on straight away.
- You can also wear or remove a title from **Flair** in the menu.

## Getting passed

If another player passes you on the week's board, you'll find out the next time you open the game. A banner above the trophy button says who passed you and how many points you need to take your place back. Tap it to open the board. Playing a battle also clears the banner.

The board tells you how many points you need to reach the next prize tier. During the **last 48 hours** of the week, it also shows how close the player below you is.

## Tabs

- **WEEK**: this week, live.
- **LAST**: last week's final standings, the players who hold titles now.
- **TODAY**: on a Daily Battle post only, that post's own ladder.
- **PRIZES**: the titles, what each one pays, and these rules.
