# Weekly Leaderboard

Every campaign battle you fight, on any GearLink post, counts toward the weekly leaderboard. Tap the **trophy button** on the map to open it.

You're ranked twice with the same points: inside a small **group** of players from your division, and across the **whole subreddit**.

## Weekly points

Your weekly points are **your best battle score at each location this week, added up**.

- Deeper locations and higher ascensions score more for the same play, so climbing pays.
- Only a location's **best** counts. Replaying a fight only adds what it beats your old best by, so grinding one spot does very little.
- Losing battles still score. Points come from damage, waves cleared and your best link.

The board resets every **Monday at 00:00 UTC**, the same time as duel leagues and weekly quests.

## Divisions and groups

There are five divisions: **Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond**. Everyone starts in Bronze.

Your first battle of the week puts you in a **group of up to 20 players** from your division. Groups fill in the order players show up.

At the reset:

- The **top quarter** of your group (up to 5 players) moves **up** a division. You need 3+ battles in the week to be promoted.
- The **bottom quarter** (up to 5) moves **down**. Groups with fewer than 8 players don't send anyone down.
- Everyone else stays where they are.

Your group's table shows a green line for the promotion zone and a red line for the drop zone.

**Missing weeks:** skip one week and you keep your division. Each week you miss after that drops you one division.

## Group prizes

Everyone in a group who plays **3 or more battles** gets paid at the reset, based on where they finished in the group. Higher divisions pay more for the same place.

| Group finish | Bronze | Silver | Gold | Platinum | Diamond | Needs               |
| ------------ | ------ | ------ | ---- | -------- | ------- | ------------------- |
| #1           | 200    | 250    | 300  | 400      | 500     | 3+ players in group |
| Top 25%      | 120    | 150    | 180  | 240      | 300     | 8+ players          |
| Top 50%      | 80     | 100    | 120  | 160      | 200     | 4+ players          |
| Everyone     | 50     | 65     | 75   | 100      | 125     | -                   |

All amounts are coins. If your group is too small for a tier, you drop to the next one down.

The **first time** you reach a division you also get a one-off bonus:

| Division | First-time bonus |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Silver   | Base Pack        |
| Gold     | Bronze Pack      |
| Platinum | Silver Pack      |
| Diamond  | Gold Pack        |

## Subreddit titles

The top 10 across the whole subreddit also take a **title**: a subreddit flair only they can wear, for the week after. When that week ends the flair comes off, unless you earn it again. Title prizes are paid on top of your group prize.

| Rank  | Title flair     | Prize                   | Needs                  |
| ----- | --------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| #1    | Weekly Champion | 600 coins + Silver Pack | 3+ players on the week |
| #2-3  | Weekly Top 3    | 400 coins + Bronze Pack | 6+ players             |
| #4-10 | Weekly Top 10   | 250 coins + Base Pack   | 15+ players            |

- Prizes land in your bag the first time you open the game after the reset. A notice tells you where you finished, whether you moved division, and **WEAR IT** puts a title flair on straight away.
- You can also wear or remove a title from **Flair** in the menu.

## Getting passed

If someone in your group passes you, you'll find out the next time you open the game. A banner above the trophy button says who passed you and how many points you need to take your place back. Tap it to open the board. Playing a battle also clears the banner.

The board tells you how many points you need to reach the promotion zone, or to climb out of the drop zone. During the **last 48 hours** of the week, it also shows how close the player below you is.

## Tabs

- **GROUP**: your group this week, live, with the promotion and drop zones.
- **ALL**: the whole subreddit this week, live.
- **LAST**: last week's subreddit standings, the players who hold titles now.
- **TODAY**: on a Daily Battle post only, that post's own ladder.
- **PRIZES**: group prizes for your division, the divisions, the titles, and these rules.
