# Duels & Leagues

Duels are a different fight. **No monsters and no waves.** Two boards, two HP pools, and everything you link either hits your opponent or buries their grid.

## How a duel works

- Both sides start with **60 HP**.
- **There are no turns.** You and your opponent link on the same clock, so a fast reader gets more moves in.
- A match lasts **3 minutes**.
- Your board is the big one at the bottom. Theirs is above.

| Link | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 🔴 Attack | Hits their HP directly |
| 🔵 Block | Soaks the next hit coming back at you |
| 🟢 Heal | Restores your own HP |
| **Any link of 5+** | Dumps **junk** into their grid |

Gear Power, link multipliers, hero perks and riders all work the same as in the campaign. **Frost** works differently: it chokes their sabotage, so their next link sends no junk.

## Junk

| Link length | Junk sent |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | 1 |
| 6 | 2 |
| 7 | 3 |
| 8 | 4 |
| 9+ | 5 (max) |

- Junk lands as a mound centred on the column **where your link ended**, so where you finish a link decides where it lands on them.
- Junk **can't be linked**.
- Junk is **armoured**. It only cracks when a clear happens **directly beside it** (up, down, left or right, not diagonal). **Two cracks** break it, or one **bomb** straight through.

## How to win

- Drop their HP to 0.
- **Buried is beaten:** if a player runs out of legal links, they lose on the spot.
- **On time,** whoever has more HP wins.

## Your duel loadout

Your duel hero and five pieces are **kept separately from your campaign loadout**, so climbing the ladder never means rebuilding the run you like.

These five are what you attack with **and** what other players face when they challenge you while you're away.

## Opponents

The lobby shows **5 opponents** near your rating. They're either:

- **Real players** who have entered the league. You fight their actual loadout, hero and avatar, played by the game's AI on their behalf.
- **House bots**, which fill the lobby when there aren't enough players at your rating yet.

The AI plays better the higher up the ladder an opponent sits.

## Weekly seasons

Seasons reset every **Monday at 00:00 UTC**.

- **During the week**, trophies move you between the three levels of your tier, but **never across a tier line**.
- **At the reset:**
  - Level **3** → promotes to level 1 of the tier above.
  - Level **2** → holds.
  - Level **1** → drops to level 2 of the tier below.
  - **Knight** holds.

### Entering the league

Entering this week's league puts your five in the opponent pool and makes your duels count for **trophies and the weekly prize**. Once in, you're locked in until the Monday reset. Stay out and your duels are friendly challenges.
